Timeline Rules Settings

Targets and sequence dependencies are stored in Firestore — not used from hard-coded values.

Not loaded
SEGOT, DKAAR and ITVAD share the same base timeline engine. NGAPP adds QC milestones and CI fields. LRMLW uses its own milestone sequence and Main Time Conclusion formulas. Dependencies can use Single, MAX or MIN across multiple milestones. Save every terminal's targets and dependencies separately in Firestore so they remain independently configurable.
